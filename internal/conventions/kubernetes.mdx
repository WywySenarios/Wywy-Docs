## Kubeconfig

All kubeconfigs and files under kubeconfigs and must have file permissions fit under umask 077 or similar (i.e. x00).

No script may replace an already existing kubeconfig folder or file.

### Individual Clusters

Individual repo kubeconfig must be located in `~/.kube/configs/<repo-name>`.

There **must** be an entrypoint for kubectl that injects the `KUBECONFIG` environment variable.

### Dev & Prod Clusters

The dev and prod cluster kubeconfigs must be located in `~/.kube/config/`.
